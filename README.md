## Before Solving a Problem

1. **Understand the problem clearly.**  
   Identify the goal, conditions, and input constraints.

2. **Work through small examples by hand.**  
   Observe how you reach the answer.

3. **Start with a correct brute-force solution.**  
   Prioritize correctness before efficiency.

4. **Identify the bottleneck.**  
   Look for repeated calculations and unnecessary searches.

5. **Find ways to improve efficiency.**  
   Consider reusing results, eliminating candidates, or choosing a suitable data structure.

6. **Justify why your approach works.**  
   Explain why it cannot miss the correct answer.

7. **Test edge cases and check complexity.**  
   Verify correctness and confirm that time and space usage meet the constraints.
